About Me

Savoir, penser, rêver. Je pense, donc je suis.

Hi, I'm Jasmine Zheng! I'm a student, aspiring physician, writer, dancer, and pianist fascinated by the human mind.

I love exploring the space where science and creativity meet, whether I'm researching neuroscience and sleep, writing stories, making art, or dancing. I'm always asking questions, trying new things, and finding connections between seemingly different interests.

This website is a little collection of the things I've created, discovered, and cared about along the way. I hope you find something here that sparks your curiosity, too!
